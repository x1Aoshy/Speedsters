# Egg Mobile: what the code needs (for Astra)

References: `reference/` (`16.webp` side view, `17.png` rear/cockpit view, `18.png` Eggman logo).
Our current version is `preview/EggMobile_preview.blend`, generated by `audit/egg_mobile_bpy.py`.
You can start from it or make a new one. If you follow these points, the game uses it as is.

## 1. What to hand over
- The **.blend**, with the pieces named as in section 3. Save it in `design/npc/eggmobile/astra/`.
- I export the FBX and the manifest (`eggmobile_manifest.json`) from your .blend. You don't need to
  export anything, but if you send an FBX as well, even better.

## 2. Axes, scale and origin
- **1 Blender unit = 1 Roblox stud.** Total size about 11 × 9 studs, 7 tall. Eggman is R6: torso 2
  wide, about 5 tall standing.
- **Front of the ship = +Y in Blender**, which is -Z in Roblox. That is where the glass bubble, the
  dashboard and the levers are. The engine and exhaust go at the back (-Y in Blender). The current
  .blend already works like this: the screen is at Blender y = +2.24 and the fan at y = -3.92.
- **Origin (0,0,0):** the floor point under the centre of the ship. The ship floats: its lowest point
  must be at least 0.2 above 0. Today the bottom of the black sphere is at 0.6.
- Apply transforms (Ctrl+A, All Transforms) to every piece before handing it over: position, rotation
  and scale at identity, with the origin of every piece at (0,0,0).

Positions in Roblox coordinates (x, height, z). In Blender that is (x, -z, height).

| Eggman's seat (HumanoidRootPart) | (0, 5.55, 0.45) | |

| Right lever, base | (1.7, 5.12, -0.63) | same |

If you move any of these, tell me the new numbers: I adjust the animations and the tests.

## 5. Limits
- **Triangles:** each piece at most 20,000 triangles and 10,000 vertices; whole ship about 40,000
  max. It's a single ship in the lobby.
- **Smooth shading:** clean normals (Shade Auto Smooth / Weighted Normal). No holes or flipped faces.
- **Nothing else:** no textures, lights, cameras or modifiers left unapplied in the pieces.
